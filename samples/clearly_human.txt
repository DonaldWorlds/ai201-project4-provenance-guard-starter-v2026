I spent most of Saturday trying to fix the back gate and failed. The hinge had rusted through on one side, so the whole thing sagged and caught on the path every time it swung. My neighbour came out halfway through — he does this, he waits until you are committed — looked at it for a while, and said the post was the real problem. He was right. I had been sanding a hinge while the thing holding it up quietly rotted. We are replacing the post next weekend, assuming it does not rain again, assuming I can get the old one out, assuming a great many things. The gate is currently tied shut with a dog lead.
