I want to write about my first job. I worked in a small shop near my house. The owner was a kind man. He taught me how to count money and how to talk to customers. I was very shy at first. I did not like to speak to people I did not know. But after some weeks it became easier. I learned many new words in English there. Some customers were patient with me and some were not. The kind ones helped me more than any class did. I still think about that shop. It closed two years ago. I was sad when I heard the news because that place changed me.
